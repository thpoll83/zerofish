# Test video frames

`passage1/` holds 124 frames of a game video recorded by the repository owner on the board the
app is meant for (the same wooden board and pieces as the `own_*.jpg` photos in `../boards/`),
filmed hand-held from White's side at a low angle. The frames were extracted at 4 frames per
second and downscaled to 360x640. `frames.txt` lists them with the frame rate, the moves that
had been played when the passage starts and the moves played during it. They are used only as
unit-test inputs.
